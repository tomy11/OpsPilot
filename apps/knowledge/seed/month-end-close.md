# Finance Month-End Close

## Timeline
All branch sales must be reconciled against POS settlement reports by working day 2 of the new month.
The general ledger is locked on working day 5; late journal entries need CFO approval.

## Inventory valuation
Run the inventory valuation report after the last stock count and post variance above 1% to the variance account.
