# IT Password and Account Policy

## Password rules
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and must not reuse any of the last 5 passwords.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN and the ERP system.

## Lost or compromised account
If you think your account is compromised, reset your password immediately and report it to the IT helpdesk at extension 1234.
IT will never ask for your password by email or chat.
